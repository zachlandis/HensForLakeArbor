LAKE ARBOR FILING 6

Should Lake Arbor homeowners be allowed to keep a few backyard hens?

A proposed update to the Filing 6 covenants would allow homeowners to responsibly keep a small number of backyard hens while maintaining reasonable protections for neighbors.

See the proposal

What would be allowed?

  • Up to 6 hens
  • Backyard coop or enclosure
  • Personal household use
  • Responsible chicken keeping
  • Compliance with City of Arvada requirements

What would not be allowed?

  • No roosters
  • No commercial chicken operations
  • No other livestock
  • No unreasonable noise or odor
  • No unsanitary or nuisance conditions

Why update the covenant?

Fresh eggs

A small flock can provide fresh eggs for local families.

Less food waste

Hens can consume many household food scraps that would otherwise be discarded.

Useful compost

Chicken bedding and manure can become valuable compost material.

More homeowner choice

This proposal does not require anyone to own chickens. It simply gives Filing 6 homeowners the option.

How the amendment works

Lake Arbor Filing No. 6 contains 377 lots.

Under Section 17 of the Filing 6 Declaration, a majority of lot owners must approve an amendment.

At least 189 Filing 6 lots must approve the change.

The amendment would apply only to Lake Arbor Filing No. 6.

Proposed amendment

Section 11 would be amended to allow the limited keeping of up to six female chickens, or hens, on residential lots within Filing 6, subject to restrictions including no roosters, personal noncommercial use, secure rear-yard enclosure, reasonable sanitation and nuisance standards, and compliance with applicable City of Arvada requirements.

Final amendment language is subject to review before signature collection begins.

Frequently asked questions

Will roosters be allowed?

No. The proposed amendment would allow hens only.

Does this allow other livestock?

No. The proposed change is limited specifically to backyard hens.

Will chickens be allowed to roam freely?

No. The proposal requires hens to be kept in a secure backyard coop, run, or other appropriate enclosure.

Does signing mean I have to own chickens?

No. Approval simply gives homeowners the option to keep hens responsibly.

Does this affect all of Lake Arbor?

No. This proposed amendment applies only to Filing 6.
