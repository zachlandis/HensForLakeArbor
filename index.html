THE AMENDMENT

A small change. Clear safeguards.

This amendment does not remove Lake Arbor's livestock restrictions. It creates one narrow exception for responsibly kept backyard hens within Filing 6.

✓

What would be allowed

  • Up to 6 female chickens
  • Secure rear-yard coop or enclosure
  • Responsible hen keeping
  • Compliance with applicable laws and regulations
✕

What would not be allowed

  • No roosters
  • No other livestock under this exception
  • No unreasonable odor or noise
  • No unsanitary or nuisance conditions

APPROVED AMENDMENT LANGUAGE

Amendment of Section 11 — Livestock and Poultry

The following is the approved amendment language for the Filing 6 signature collection process.

Pursuant to Section 17 of the Declaration applicable to Lake Arbor Filing No. 6, the undersigned owners of lots within Lake Arbor Filing No. 6 hereby approve the following amendment to Section 11 of the Declaration.

Section 11 is amended solely to create the following limited exception to the existing prohibition against livestock and poultry:

Backyard Hens Exception

Notwithstanding the existing prohibition against livestock and poultry contained in Section 11, an owner or occupant of a residential lot within Lake Arbor Filing No. 6 may keep up to six (6) female chickens (hens) on the lot, subject to the following requirements:

1. No roosters.

Roosters shall not be kept on any lot.

2. Rear-yard enclosure.

Hens shall be maintained within a secure coop, run, or other appropriate enclosure located in the rear yard of the lot.

3. Sanitation and nuisance.

The keeping of hens shall not create an unreasonable odor, noise, sanitation issue, or other nuisance affecting neighboring properties.

4. Compliance with applicable law.

The keeping of hens and any associated coop, run, enclosure, or structure shall comply with all applicable governmental laws, ordinances, regulations, and requirements.

Except as expressly modified by this amendment, Section 11 and all other provisions of the Declaration applicable to Lake Arbor Filing No. 6 shall remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

FAQ

Common questions

Will roosters be allowed?

No. The amendment is limited to female chickens, or hens. Roosters would remain prohibited.

Will chickens be allowed to roam freely?

No. The amendment requires hens to be maintained within a secure coop, run, or other appropriate enclosure located in the rear yard.

What about smell?

The amendment specifically states that keeping hens may not create an unreasonable odor, sanitation issue, or other nuisance affecting neighboring properties.

What about noise?

Roosters would remain prohibited, and the amendment states that keeping hens may not create unreasonable noise affecting neighboring properties.

Will this attract rats or pests?

Coop and feed areas will need to be managed responsibly. The amendment prohibits unreasonable sanitation issues and other nuisance conditions affecting neighboring properties.

Does signing mean I have to own chickens?

No. Signing means you support giving Filing 6 homeowners the choice to keep hens under the requirements of the amendment.

Does this affect the entire Lake Arbor neighborhood?

No. This amendment would apply only to Lake Arbor Filing No. 6.

How many homeowners need to approve the amendment?

Filing 6 contains 377 lots. Approval from a majority requires at least 189 lots. The campaign goal is 220 signed lots to provide a margin above the minimum requirement.

Who is organizing this?

This is a homeowner-led effort organized by Zach Landis, a Filing 6 resident.
