THE PROPOSAL

A small change. Clear safeguards.

This proposal does not remove Lake Arbor's livestock restrictions. It creates one narrow exception for responsibly kept backyard hens.

✓

What would be allowed

  • Up to 6 female chickens
  • Backyard coop or enclosure
  • Personal household use
  • Responsible hen keeping
  • Compliance with City of Arvada rules
✕

What would not be allowed

  • No roosters
  • No commercial chicken operations
  • No other livestock
  • No unreasonable odor or noise
  • No unsanitary or nuisance conditions

HOW IT WORKS

Filing 6 homeowners have the authority to amend their covenants.

377 Filing 6 lots
189 minimum approvals
220 campaign goal

Section 17 of the Filing 6 Declaration allows the covenants to be amended when an instrument approving the change is signed by a majority of the then-owners of the lots.

This proposal would apply only to Lake Arbor Filing No. 6.

PROPOSED AMENDMENT

The amendment language

The proposed amendment would permit the limited keeping of up to six female chickens, or hens, on residential lots within Filing 6, subject to restrictions including no roosters, personal noncommercial use, secure rear-yard enclosure, reasonable sanitation and nuisance standards, and compliance with applicable City of Arvada requirements.

Final legal language will be posted here after review and before signature collection begins.

FAQ

Common questions

Will roosters be allowed?

No. The proposal is limited to female chickens, or hens.

Will chickens be allowed to roam freely?

No. The proposal requires hens to be kept in a secure backyard coop, run, or appropriate enclosure.

What about smell?

The proposal requires reasonable sanitation and prohibits nuisance conditions. Properly maintained small backyard flocks should not create unreasonable odor.

What about noise?

Roosters, which are the primary source of loud chicken noise, would remain prohibited.

Will this attract rats or pests?

Feed and coop areas must be managed responsibly. Poor sanitation or nuisance conditions would not be permitted under the proposed amendment.

Does signing mean I have to own chickens?

No. Signing means you support giving Filing 6 homeowners the choice to keep hens under the proposed restrictions.

Does this affect the entire Lake Arbor neighborhood?

No. This amendment would apply only to Filing 6.

Who is organizing this?

This is a homeowner-led effort organized by Zach Landis, a Filing 6 resident.
